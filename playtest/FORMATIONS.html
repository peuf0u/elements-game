Wind 3 held back is the strongest formation found. It rates about 60 points above the other two, and it also came first in the search's own round robin. In the pairings above, 80 games had Mirrored and reshaped beating it 65%; with 200 games that turned out to be luck.

No sure win

No pairing is lopsided. The biggest gaps in the pairings were about 65 to 35 in 80 games, and the head-to-head shows such gaps shrink with more games. Choosing a formation matters a little without deciding the game.

What this suggests

  • A new default: Wind 3 held back. It is the strongest formation found, and it changes only four pieces of yours.
  • A set of presets: the top six are within about 10 points of each other and look different from one another.
  • Your default's weak spot is probably Wind 3 standing exposed on b3, which Wind 3 held back tucks away.
