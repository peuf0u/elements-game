How to read the pieces
Fire · straight Wind · diagonal Water · river Earth · any way, can't capture

The shape shows how a piece moves. Size = dots (1–3) and piece size. The rim and the colour shade show the owner: lighter colours are White's, deeper ones Black's. The star is the Wizard. A small shape sitting on a bigger one is a combined piece; the outline underneath is the bigger element, or the attacker when Earth has joined it. Tap or drag a piece; dots show where it can go, a red ring is a capture, a gold + is a combine. Tap an opponent's piece to see where it can reach.
